Engineering Integrity for Global Infrastructure Diagnostics

Our Multi-Disciplinary Engineering Facility

Unlike traditional distributors who operate solely as sales intermediaries, PTS operates a fully equipped, domestic hardware laboratory and data science facility. When an NSV vehicle experiences intermittent CAN-bus packet drops, or an FWD geophone tower reports noisy deflection waveforms, our diagnostic engineers trace the issue from the physical copper conductors to the DSP filter algorithms.

We maintain an inventory of over 1,200 specialized industrial connectors (Amphenol MIL-DTL-38999, Lemo push-pull, Deutsch DT, and IP69K washdown housings), enabling us to fabricate drop-in replacement harnesses in 24 to 48 hours without waiting for international OEM shipments.
